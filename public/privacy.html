Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

SEVAK ("we", "us", "our") is a voice-first Indian document assistant that helps you understand confusing legal notices, bank letters, and government documents in simple language. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

  • Uploaded documents: When you upload a PDF or image (notice, challan, bank letter, KYC, etc.), we process the extracted text to generate a plain-language explanation. Documents are processed in-memory and are not stored permanently on our servers.
  • Voice recordings: When you use the microphone, audio is sent to our speech-to-text service to transcribe your question. Audio is not stored after transcription.
  • Usage data: We may collect anonymized, aggregate usage statistics to improve the service (e.g., which document categories are most common). No personally identifiable information is retained.
  • Cookies: We use a cookie-consent preference cookie to remember your banner choice. No tracking or advertising cookies are set by default.

2. How we use your information

  • To explain your document and generate next steps, deadlines, and citations.
  • To transcribe and answer your voice questions about an uploaded document.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve SEVAK.

3. Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your documents, voice recordings, or personal data. Document text and transcripts may be sent to third-party AI/speech services (e.g., Gnani AI Prisma STT, Timbre TTS, Hugging Face Evon) solely to deliver the feature you requested. Those processors are bound to use the data only to provide the service.

4. Data retention

  • Uploaded documents: ephemeral, processed in-memory, released after the request.
  • Voice recordings: deleted immediately after transcription.
  • Transcripts/answers: kept only for the duration of your browser session; cleared when you reset or close.

5. Your rights

You may refuse to upload a document or use the microphone at any time. You can delete your session data by refreshing the page or clicking "Naya Document / Back to Home". Where applicable, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Security

We use HTTPS in production and restrict access to document processing. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. Do not upload documents containing highly sensitive identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, bank account numbers, passwords) unless necessary.

7. Not legal advice

SEVAK is an AI document-assistant, not a lawyer. It explains clauses in simple language and does not make binding legal predictions or guarantee legal outcomes. For any legal dispute, consult a qualified advocate or legal-aid clinic.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of SEVAK after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Write to us at privacy@sevak.example.com.
